How it works

A Material Passport is a record that follows a recovered material from its original mission to its next use. It stores where the piece came from, what it is made of, its condition, its original function, possible second lives and the tests required before reuse. That turns reuse into traceability instead of guesswork. This page is a shared database of those passports.

1. **Create:** press **+ New passport**, fill in the fields (mission, component, material and so on), pick a status and optionally attach a photo, then **Save passport**.
2. **Find:** use the search box or the status dropdown to narrow the list. The counters at the top show totals per status.
3. **Open:** click any row to see the full record with its photo on the right.
4. **Update:** inside a record you can change the status, add or replace the photo, or delete it.
